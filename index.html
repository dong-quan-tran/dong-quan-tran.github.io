NETWORK PRIVACY RESEARCH / UTA REU

Website fingerprinting
and privacy defenses

At UTA's Information Security Lab, I research how traffic patterns can reveal which websites a person visits. My Tamaraw experiments evaluate privacy defenses against four website fingerprinting attacks.

Tamaraw Phases 1 and 2 were part of UTA's Classic CSE Department REU Program in Spring and Summer 2026, under Dr. Rémi Chou.
